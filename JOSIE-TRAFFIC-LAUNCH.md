# Josie → The Sports Genius: 7-Day No-Spend Traffic Pilot

**Goal:** Turn genuinely interested viewers into users of live scores, research matchups, saved games and timestamped predictions. No paid ads, purchased traffic, artificial engagement or gambling outcome promises.

## Publish cadence (manual review and posting; not scheduled or sent automatically)
- Day 1: Josie reaction Short. Tease the funny reaction, offer live scores. TikTok link and YouTube Shorts score link.
- Day 2: Post one screenshot/video of a genuine, up-to-date game score; invite a favorite-team discussion, not betting tips.
- Day 3: Facebook game-day post; share a relevant matchup page and a question.
- Day 4: Share a Josie behind-the-scenes moment; use the Instagram Josie URL.
- Day 5: Show a publicly timestamped prediction and graded outcome including misses. Avoid cherry-picked claims.
- Day 6: Ask followers to save a matchup or follow their team. Keep the message useful rather than spammy.
- Day 7: Compare dashboard campaign, page, source, and action counts. Choose the next two proven formats before spending on promotion.
