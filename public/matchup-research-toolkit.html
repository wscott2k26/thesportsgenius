Research the matchup before you chase the headline.

Use this repeatable checklist to organize the basics: team quality, availability, scheduling context, recent form, venue, market movement, and source quality. It measures research completeness — not whether a pick will win.

MATCHUP

Team strength & record

Compare overall and situational records instead of relying on one headline stat.

Availability

Confirm injuries, suspensions, probable starters, and late status changes from current sources.

Rest & travel

Back-to-backs, short rest, travel and schedule density can change the context of a matchup.

Recent form

Review recent performance while avoiding tiny-sample overreaction.

Matchup factors

Look for style, pace, efficiency, unit-vs-unit, weather or other sport-specific interactions.

Market context

If you examine odds or lines, record what changed and when rather than treating a screenshot as permanent.

Source quality

Separate official/team/league data from rumors and secondary commentary.

Decision discipline

Write down what would change your view and avoid pretending uncertainty disappeared.

YOUR NOTES

Keep the reasoning separate from the result. That makes it easier to review whether the process was sound later.

WHY THIS TOOL EXISTS

A repeatable process beats memory.

Research gets sloppy when people remember only the factors that support the opinion they already wanted. A simple checklist creates a record of what you actually checked, what is still unknown, and what would make you change your mind. Sports Genius uses data-driven models and transparent records, but no model removes uncertainty.
