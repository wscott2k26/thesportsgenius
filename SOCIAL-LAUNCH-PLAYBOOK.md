## Legitimate paid reach — NOT authorized to spend
- **Do not purchase followers, bot views, click farms or artificial engagement.**
- YouTube Promote/Google Ads and Meta Ads are legitimate paid distribution, but the landing website's sports betting/picks/affiliate content may be classified as **gambling-promoting content** even if a video itself is educational. Before any campaign: confirm policy certification, approved regions, age and legal restrictions, landing page disclaimers, and eligible account status.
- YouTube official policy says views and subscribers from paid Promote campaigns do **not** count toward the YouTube Partner Program entry thresholds. Focus on people who watch future videos organically.
- **Pilot only after compliance and a user-approved budget:** run 2 original educational creatives with distinct tagged links. Optimize for engaged visits, repeat visitors and newsletter/team follows — **not raw paid views**. Pause underperforming ads.
- Do not claim real costs per click, views, subscribers, revenue or guaranteed ROI without actual campaign data.
